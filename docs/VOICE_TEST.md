# Voice Test Studio

1. **Use headphones.** Open speakers and a live mic will feed back.
2. Choose microphone and monitor output (or System default) and press **Start monitoring**. A red **LIVE MONITORING** indicator shows in the sidebar while it runs.
3. Toggle **Original / Processed** to A/B your voice. Watch the four meters: mic input, DSP input, DSP output, monitor output.
4. **Emergency stop** (button or hotkey) halts monitoring instantly.
5. **Record a test** (5–30 s): a **RECORDING** indicator is shown, audio stays in memory, and you can play it back through any preset or compare several presets on the same take. Nothing is saved or uploaded unless you export.

Latency shown is the measured software buffer path (see [AUDIO_ENGINE.md](AUDIO_ENGINE.md)). Recording never starts automatically.
